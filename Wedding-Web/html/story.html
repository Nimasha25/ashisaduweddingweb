Our Love Story

Once upon a time... In the serendipitous tapestry of life, amidst the vibrant corridors of campus, a message arrived—a whisper of destiny. A brother of my father's esteemed friend, a conduit of fate, conveyed that a kindred spirit was inquiring about me, a presence cloaked in mystery and anticipation. With a heart both hopeful and trepidatious, I found myself yearning to uncover the identity of this stranger who beckoned from afar. As the sun dipped below the horizon on that fateful Saturday, July 2nd, 2022, at precisely naer night , he reached out, initiating a dialogue that would intertwine our souls. "How does our story begin?" he inquired, his words resonating with the promise of uncharted territories. On July 3rd, in a bold overture, he sought the blessing of my mother, proposing a conversation that would transcend mere acquaintance into the realm of something deeper—a communion of hearts. With maternal encouragement, I ventured forth, eager yet apprehensive, to unveil the essence of this enigmatic suitor. As the days turned into a crescendo of emotions, on the end of that month, he declared, “I have fallen in love,” words that hung in the air like a sweet melody, echoing the sentiments of countless hearts before us. Yet, there was an undeniable yearning within him to truly know me, to grasp the intricacies of my being. Driven by an intoxicating blend of longing and determination, he secured an air ticket, a passage not merely through the skies but across the fabric of our destinies. On January end, 2023, he embarked on a journey to the shores of Sri Lanka, culminating in a breathtaking surprise on January 30th, as he materialized before me at my campus, his gaze warm and profound, adorned with the hues of chocolate brown. In that moment, our love story began—not just as two individuals converging but as two souls destined to intertwine, crafting a narrative rich with promise, laughter, and the unwavering spirit of companionship.
